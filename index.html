My journey: from the classroom to education diplomacy

Trang’s path into education did not begin in a classroom. It began in accounting and finance.

  1. 2013Graduates in Accounting and Finance, Banking Academy of Vietnam.
  2. 2015Starts teaching English as a freelance teacher and finds her calling.
  3. 2016Trains in TESOL and begins training teachers with Educap – Australian International TESOL, in cities and provinces across Vietnam.
  4. 2019 to 2022Brings classroom practice into EdTech and publishing: FLYER, Twinkl and ClassIn.
  5. 2021Founds the Mentoring Program for Professional Development of Educators.
  6. 2022Moves to Canada.
  7. 2023Completes an MA in TESOL at Horizons University (France), on a 50% scholarship.
  8. 2024Co-authors a book chapter on virtual mentoring (IGI Global) and founds CVCEC in December.
  9. 2026Launches the Viet Youth Readiness Hub and Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em, completes the University of Toronto’s Leading Urban Change program, and begins Government Relations studies at Seneca Polytechnic.

Work and impact

CVCEC and the work behind it

Trang founded the Canada Vietnam Cultural & Educational Council (CVCEC), a federally incorporated non-profit based in Toronto, in December 2024. It also acts as an institutional connector between Canadian public education and education stakeholders in Vietnam, helping students and families make informed, safe decisions.

VYRH
Youth · Since April 2026

Viet Youth Readiness Hub

A flagship youth initiative of CVCEC, launched in Toronto. It helps Vietnamese students prepare for and settle into Canadian schools through orientation sessions, mentoring, civic engagement and youth leadership. It offers a bilingual Study in Canada guide for Vietnamese families.

Open the guide
Language · CVCEC

Trường Tiếng Việt Của Em

My Vietnamese School: an online platform where Vietnamese-Canadian children learn Vietnamese language and culture at home with their families. It is built by a teaching team in Ontario, Manitoba and Prince Edward Island.
